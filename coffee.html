Espresso & Grain

Crafted with Intention

At Espresso & Grain, we believe that coffee is more than a beverage; it is a ritual. Born from a passion for the unadulterated bean, our journey began in pursuit of the perfect extraction.

We partner directly with small-lot farmers who share our dedication to sustainable and ethical practices. Every batch is roasted in-house to coax out the nuanced terroir, resulting in a cup that is both complex and deeply comforting.
